Our answer: RescueNexus

  1. Report

    A citizen picks the emergency, marks the spot on a map and adds a photo. It takes about 30 seconds.

  2. Respond

    The control room sees every case on a live map, most urgent first, and sends the right team.

  3. Alert

    Everyone living nearby gets a warning by SMS and email. The reporter gets updates.

Why customers will choose us

  • Report in 30 seconds

    Three steps: type of emergency, spot on the map, photo. No long forms.

  • Most urgent first

    Words like "people trapped" or "unconscious" mark a case as critical, so it is never missed.

  • The whole area is warned

    One click alerts every resident within a chosen distance.

  • Works by simple SMS

    No smartphone or internet? A text message is enough to report.

  • A record that holds up

    Time, place and photo are saved permanently. Useful for police complaints, insurance claims and accountability.

  • Free for every citizen

    Organisations cover the cost, so anyone can ask for help.

We support 112, we don't replace it. A call reaches one person. We also send a photo, the exact spot and a warning to the neighbourhood.
